Organizations let you collaborate with your team on Runpod. Members share access to resources, and admins control who can do what through roles and permissions.

## What you can manage

**Roles and permissions** — assign roles to org members to control access to pods, endpoints, billing, and org settings. See [Roles and permissions](/accounts-billing/organizations/roles-and-permissions).

**SSH key management** — each member adds their own SSH public key in their Runpod account settings. When your organization deploys a Pod, Runpod injects the public keys of all current members into that Pod so any member can connect with their matching private key. See [SSH key management](/accounts-billing/organizations/SSH-key-management).

**Resource tagging** — create and apply key/value tags to Pods, Endpoints, Network Volumes, and Clusters to organize resources by team, project, or environment. See [Resource tagging](/accounts-billing/organizations/resource-tagging).

## Managing your organization

To manage your organization's members and settings, navigate to **Settings → Organization** in the [Runpod console](https://console.runpod.io).
