After your compute reaches **Online**, anyone in your organization can deploy Pods on it. You use the same deploy flow you already use for Runpod cloud. The only difference is where you pick the GPU.

## Requirements

- Private compute with the **Online** status. See [Set up private compute](/hybrid-cloud/setup).
- Membership in the organization that owns the compute, with permission to deploy Pods. See [Roles](/organizations/org-roles).

## Deploy a Pod

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Pods page">
    In the Runpod console, make sure your organization is selected, then go to **Pods** and click **Deploy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your private compute">
    In the **Compute** section, select the **Private compute** tab. It lists the GPU types available on your organization's online compute. Select the GPU you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure and deploy">
    Configure the rest of the Pod the way you normally would, including the template, storage, and environment variables. Then click **Deploy**. For details on each Pod setting, see [Manage Pods](/pods/manage-pods).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
You can also start from the **Hybrid Cloud** page: click **Deploy Pod** next to any compute with the **Online** status.
</Tip>

## How placement works

Pods only run on your compute when you select the **Private compute** tab in the GPU picker. Standard deploys always use Runpod compute.

When you deploy on private compute, Runpod places the Pod on your organization's online compute that matches the GPU you selected. Pods deployed on your compute stay within your organization. Other Runpod customers can't deploy to them.

## Track usage

The **Hybrid Cloud** page shows how many GPUs on your compute are in use, so you can see available capacity before you deploy.
