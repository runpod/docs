Runpod Hybrid Cloud lets you bring hardware you already own under the same console, CLI, and APIs your team uses on Runpod. Your compute runs first, and Runpod cloud handles overflow when you need it.

## Requirements

Runpod Hybrid Cloud is available to organizations only. The Runpod team must enable External Compute for your org account before the feature appears in your console. [Contact us](https://www.runpod.io/everywhere) to get set up.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your machine">
    In the console, go to **Resources → External compute** and click **Add compute**. Select your machine type (on-prem server, AWS EC2 instance, or any cloud VM), give it a name, and click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the host agent">
    Copy the install command that appears and paste it into an SSH session on your machine. This installs the Runpod host agent, a small program that connects your machine to Runpod over an outbound-only encrypted connection. No inbound ports are opened. Set your public and private IPs, open the required firewall ports, then reboot. For full instructions, see [Set up external compute](/hybrid-cloud/setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for verification">
    Runpod runs automated hardware checks in the background. You don't need to do anything. When verification is complete, the machine's status changes to **Online** and its GPU type appears in the console.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy from the same console">
    Your team deploys Pods exactly as they would for any Runpod machine. An **External Compute** section appears in the GPU picker showing your available hardware. The deploy experience is identical to standard Runpod Pods. See [Deploy Pods on your external compute](/hybrid-cloud/deploy). External compute usage is tracked in the console so you can see how many GPUs are in use at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

**One control plane across owned and cloud compute**

Manage the hardware you own and Runpod cloud as a single estate. No scheduling scripts, access tickets, or per-silo tooling.

**Consistent developer experience**

Your team gets the same Runpod console, CLI, and APIs on the hardware you own. SSH still works.

**Your hardware, your data, your boundary**

You own the hardware and the data on it. Runpod orchestrates it without taking ownership. Everything you onboard is visible only to your organization.

**No lock-in**

The host agent uninstalls cleanly. Your workloads are standard containers.

## Who it's for

Runpod Hybrid Cloud is built for teams that own GPU infrastructure and want to put it to work without replatforming.

- **Research institutions and universities**: Give researchers self-service access to the institution's GPUs, with Runpod cloud absorbing overflow when the queue is full.
- **Enterprise teams**: Unify on-prem clusters, hyperscaler reservations, and Runpod cloud under one control plane with org-scoped governance.
- **AI-native startups**: Scale on owned hardware first and burst to cloud without changing how your team deploys.

## Security

The host agent connects outbound only over an encrypted overlay. No inbound ports are opened. Everything onboarded is scoped to your organization and not visible to other customers. For security reviews, you get a direct line to Runpod engineers to walk the architecture.

## Get started

Runpod Hybrid Cloud is available to early customers, and the Runpod team works with you directly to set up your machines. [Request a demo](https://www.runpod.io/everywhere) to get started.
