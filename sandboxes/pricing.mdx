Sandboxes run on CPU compute, and the price depends on the size you choose. To set the size, see [CPU size](/sandboxes/configure#cpu-size).

Sandboxes are billed per second from creation until they terminate, at the rate in `compute.costPerHr` (USD per hour). The default size, 2 vCPU and 4 GB of memory, costs **$0.14 per hour**. Your account needs enough balance to start a sandbox, otherwise create returns `402`.

Sandbox spend appears as its own line in your billing summary and CSV export.

## Keep costs down

- **Terminate sandboxes when you're done,** including in error-handling code. A sandbox bills until it terminates.
- **Set a short idle timeout** for interactive or agent work, so forgotten sandboxes stop on their own. See [Timeouts](/sandboxes/lifecycle#timeouts).
- **Use the smallest size that works.** Cost scales with vCPUs and memory.
- **Watch for exited containers.** A sandbox whose main process has exited can keep billing until it's terminated.

Volumes are billed separately. See [Storage](/sandboxes/storage).
